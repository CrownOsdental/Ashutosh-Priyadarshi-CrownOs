About me

I'm a 14-year-old Class 10 student at DAV, Patna, and the founder of CrownOS. I'm building software focused on clinic management and patient retention, with the goal of helping clinics manage their operations and build stronger relationships with patients.

I'm currently working with a real clinic, using direct feedback from healthcare professionals to understand their problems and improve the product based on actual requirements rather than assumptions.

I was also invited to the Bihar AI Summit, where I had the opportunity to interact with founders, entrepreneurs and professionals from the technology ecosystem.

These experiences have made me particularly interested in entrepreneurship, SaaS, healthcare technology, product development, customer discovery and business.
